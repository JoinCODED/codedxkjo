Topic 06 · Story · their own list, three hours later

You did not need me for that.

This morning I gave you one number and asked whether it was good. Here is what you asked me for, in your own words, at ten past nine.

You already knew what was missing. What you have not had is a name for it, and a habit around it.
The term

A number becomes information when you put something beside it.

1
The same thing last periodIs this getting better or worse?A short period hides a trend. A long one hides a turn.
2
The targetAre we where we said we would be?If nobody set a target, say so. Do not invent one.
3
Another groupWho is doing this better, and what do they do differently?The groups have to be alike enough to compare.
4
A benchmark outsideIs this normal for work like ours?Where did it come from, and who paid for it.
And a fifth, always available

Compare against nothing, and say so.

Defensible
“257 requests, and I have no baseline yet.”
Not defensible
“257 requests, which is strong.”
One of those you can defend in a meeting.
,
The comparison you choose is a claim about what matters.Against last year says time matters. Against the branch next door says fairness across branches matters. Both are legitimate, and they lead to different decisions.
,
Comparing like with unlike is the most common failure.Industrial handles 257 and Central handles 365. Central is bigger. Raw counts say nothing until you decide whether you are comparing volume or rate.

Last periodBe careful here. Work orders raised recently have had less time to age, so a naive split makes the recent half look faster at every site.
TargetNo target exists, and that is worth writing down.
Another groupTwenty days worse than the next slowest site.
BenchmarkThe maintenance standard most plants quote is 30 days, illustrative; say where it came from or mark it unknown.

The term

An outlier is far outside the range. A signal means something.

OUTLIER OR SIGNAL The data cannot tell you which one you are holding. The business can.
1 Is it a data error, or a real event?
2 If it is real, is it a one-off or the first of a pattern?
3 Who in the business would know?
Why the instinct runs the wrong way

The cleaner chart is not the more correct chart. It is the more comfortable one.

Keeping a coding error
Inflates an average for a year, and somebody usually spots it.
Deleting a real event
Quietly breaks a budget twelve months later, and nobody traces it back.
The asymmetry is why the default is keep, and ask.
Topic 08 · Integration · nothing new after the next twenty minutes

Ahmadi Refinery takes 49.4 days to close a work order, twenty days worse than the next slowest site.

That is a true comparison, with a row count behind it. What does it mean?
Meaning is a different question

Not what it is. What changes if it is true.

So what
What does this cost or save, and roughly how much?
Who
Who does something different because of it?
What would break it
What would have to be true for this to be wrong?
Worked example, read it aloud
So whatAhmadi takes 49 days against 28 elsewhere. It has 138 work orders sitting open right now, twice as many as the next site, and forty percent of all the open work in the company.
WhoThe person who does something different is the Ahmadi maintenance planner, not the analyst.
Breaks ifThose blank close dates are a reporting habit rather than open work, which I have not checked.
Notice what that last sentence did. It did not weaken the finding. It told your reader exactly where to push, which is the only way they can trust the rest of it.
