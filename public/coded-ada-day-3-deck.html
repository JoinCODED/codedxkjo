Not a report of everything. The smallest set of charts that answers one decision.

THE MONDAY QUESTION The one decision the reader has to make. Before you build anything, write it down. If you cannot write it, you are not ready to build.
The rule that does the most work

Every chart answers exactly one question.

, If a chart answers no question, delete it.
, If it answers three, split it.
Deleting a chart is doing more work. Somebody has to decide it does not belong, and that somebody is you.

The two lines to defend, because somebody will argue

The chart follows the claim.

Pies
People read angles badly and read lengths well.A pie with more than four slices is a puzzle. A pie with thirteen is decoration. If you want composition, use a sorted bar chart and let people read lengths.
No chart
One number that matters on its own is a sentence, not a picture.A bar chart with one tall bar and five short ones is a sentence with decoration on it. Say the number in words and give it a size.
You do not pick a chart and then find something to put in it. That is how the vendor pie happened.

Names the axes. The reader has to do the work.
It is the only part of a chart everybody reads.
If your title could sit on top of any chart in the file without changing, it is not a title. It is a label.
Where the rule breaks, and it does break

Rules with no exceptions are usually rules nobody has tested.

,
Priority is not a size order.Critical, High, Normal, Low. Sorting that by size is technically correct and practically confusing, because the business already has an order for those words. Use the business order and say why out loud.
1 Start bar chart axes at zero. A truncated axis makes a two percent difference look like a crisis. The most common way an honest chart lies.
2 Put the row count on the dashboard. You have been writing it down for two days. Now it goes where the reader can see it.
3 Color is for one thing at a time. If everything is highlighted, nothing is.
Yesterday’s card says the finding in words. What does today add?

The honest limit, say it plainly

Three days does not make you a data analyst. It makes you someone who can no longer be handed a number and be quiet about it.

That is worth more than it sounds.
